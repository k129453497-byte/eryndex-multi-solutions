ABOUT ERYNDEX / 智序科技

We design work systems for everyday business.

Eryndex designs and develops systems for business collaboration, information management and access governance. We begin with real working situations and turn fragmented processes, important information and management rules into clear, sustainable ways of working.

Technology should not create more maintenance overhead. It should clarify responsibility, make handoffs continuous and keep important decisions understandable and reviewable.

THE WORK BEHIND EVERY BUSINESS

Work becomes scattered across tools, files lose a dependable way to be preserved and access drifts out of context. Businesses do not need another isolated tool. They need everyday work to become connected again.

ONE SYSTEM · THREE RESPONSIBILITIES

Work, information and trust. One business context.

Three products with distinct responsibilities, designed to connect when the business needs them to.

01

Enterprise work platform

Eryndex Space

Give work a clearer structure.

Bring projects, tasks and team knowledge into one shared context. Make ownership, progress and the next step clear to everyone.

02

Enterprise file platform

Eryndex Files

Keep information worth keeping.

From organization and sharing to versions, backup and restore, give business information a dependable lifecycle. Find what you need today and recover what mattered yesterday.

03

Enterprise security platform

Eryndex Shield

Give trust a clear boundary.

Bring identities, devices and access policies into one clear framework. Make each authorization deliberate, with changes and exceptions that can be traced.

Space organizes work, Files preserves information and Shield governs trust. They are not competing tools, but three clear responsibilities within the Eryndex approach to business operations.

HOW WE WORK

Understand first. Build with purpose. Connect when it is ready.

  1. 01

    Begin with real work

    Understand how the business collaborates, preserves information and controls access before deciding what the system should do.

  2. 02

    Give every system a clear responsibility

    Give work, information and trust clear boundaries, reducing duplicated tools and unclear ownership.

  3. 03

    Make change sustainable

    Design implementation, training and ongoing improvement together so the system becomes a lasting way of working.

ERYNDEX SERVICES

From planning and implementation to ongoing improvement.

Eryndex provides system planning, initial configuration, data migration, workflow design, user training and maintenance support. Scope is agreed around each business environment and need.
